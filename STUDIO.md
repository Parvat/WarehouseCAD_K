# Trace — Studio theme (replaces THEME.md for app chrome)

Source of truth: the mockup files in `design/studio/*.dc.html`. When this file and a mockup disagree, the mockup wins. Every value below is copied from them.

## 1. Layout model

- Canvas fills the whole window. Everything else floats on it as dark **islands**. No fixed panels, no frame.
- Islands (all `position: fixed` over the canvas, 16px from the window edges):
  - **Top-left**: logo · project name ▾ (opens layout menu) · Auto-saved. Height 44.
  - **Top-right**: undo · redo · | · Generate/Regenerate · Check (count chip) · Export (mint). Height 44.
  - **Left rail**: 48 wide, icon buttons 36×36: Floor plans, Racking, Ops, Structural, Safety, Zones, Blocks · | · Project settings. Top 76.
  - **Library flyout**: opens right of the rail (left 72, top 76, width 232). List rows 36h: hollow icon · name · hint. Footer hint line.
  - **Right card**: width **300**, top 76, right 16. Height = its content; max = down to 76px above the bottom; then scrolls inside. Same width for every tab and state. Holds Properties / Elevations / BOM tabs, Generate, Check layout, zone and group editing.
  - **Tool pill**: bottom centre, 20px from bottom. After Pan: **Section ▾** (Rectangle R · Custom shape P — the keys while its menu is open; §7a Sections). Buttons 36×36, radius 8, groups split by 1px separators. Active tool = active fill.
  - **Bottom-left**: Hub · | · zoom − 100% +. Height 40.
  - **Tally drawer** (the BOM tab's Tally; §7a BOM): docked at the bottom of the canvas, full width — left 16 to right 16, bottom 20 (the islands'); island style, radius 10; 330 high at first, its top edge dragged to resize (200 to 70% of the window), remembered. While it is open the bottom-right island (View · Layers · Fit · ?, its cards with it) stands 12px above the drawer's top edge at the right, the right card's max height ends 12px above that island, and the bottom-left island, the tool pill and the notices stand 8px above the drawer. **Focus** (its header) hides the left rail (and its flyout), the tool pill, the top islands and the right card; the plan refits above the drawer and the drawer may be dragged up to 85% of the window. Focus again or Esc restores all (a height past 70% comes back to 70%); Esc again closes the drawer.
  - **Bottom-right**: View · Layers · Fit · ?. Height 40. **Fit** (and the fit on open and after Generate) places the plan in the canvas's free area — between the left rail and the right card, above the Tally drawer when it is open — with the usual margin: nothing of the building sits under the card (an island Focus hides takes no room). **View** opens a small dark card above the island (island style, radius 10) with the display switches only — **Aisle labels**, **Checks**, **Elevation colours**, **Section names**, **Row numbers**. **Layers** opens its own card the same way: each layer's show toggle and padlock. A click outside or Esc closes either. Section names (off by default) shows every section's tag — its name and size — on the plan; off, a section's tag shows only while it is hovered or selected. Row numbers (on by default) shows the R labels; they show anyway while the Tally drawer or its Pop out is open. Every switch is remembered as before. The right card has no Layers block. **?** (and the ? key) opens the shortcut sheet the same way: every mouse and key action of §7a, grouped Select · Bays · Row group · Tools · Keys; Esc or a click outside closes it.
- **Selection bar**: dark pill (radius 12) floating just above the selected object. Contents per object type (section 6).
  - One faint hint line under its buttons (11px, muted) per state: rows selected "Shift+click to add rows · drag to move"; bays selected "Shift+click or Shift+drag to add bays"; a column on an unlocked Columns layer "Drag to move its line". The Row group's hint is in its mode bar.
  - 8px above the selection, centred on it, never over it; below it when there is no room under the top islands; kept off the rail and the right card; it follows the pan and the zoom. Not shown while another tool is on, during a drag or a placement, or while a Row group is open (its mode bar is).
- **Mode bars** (Row group, Split, Fill): dark pill at top of canvas, centred, top 76.
- **Notices**: dark pill above the tool pill, 76px from bottom, with an action (Undo) and ✕.
- **Modals** (Project settings, Rules, Export, New project): backdrop + centred dark card.

### Plan drawing rules (see design/studio/PlanDetail.dc.html)
- **One drawing at every zoom, for every object in the layout.** No switch at all: racks, uprights, flues, walls, columns, doors, zones, MHE, safety items, blocks, text objects, dimension lines and X marks are each drawn one way only, at true size. Zooming only scales them; there is never a moment where one version swaps for another, so nothing jumps, appears, disappears or changes shape while zooming. Lines that would go under 1px stay at 1px.
- **No switch at all for uprights.** Always drawn at their true width (3″ or 4″ from the frame), minimum 1px on screen. Zoomed out they just look like thin lines because they are small. There is never a moment where one version swaps for another, so nothing jumps or changes shape while zooming. Outlines 1px, non-scaling, pixel-snapped.
- Back-to-back pair = one outline with a visible flue gap between the inner faces.
- Aisle width = dimension line face to face with slim open chevron arrowheads (8px, fixed size). Value sits in a break in the line (no halo, no badge); if the aisle is too narrow for the break, value goes 5px above the line. No badge. Format `10′-6″`. Blue OK, amber `#B8730E` (text `#9A5F0A`) under pick width, red `#C0452A` (text `#B23A22`) under travel width. All aisle labels on one row; slide only to clear a column.
- Column = solid `#1F2321` square; clearance to each face = small grey `#767C74` number (9.5px) on a thin dotted `#9AA29C` line, **no arrowheads**; touching a face = number only.
- Lost positions per pallet position (not per bay): a fixed-size square X only, `#C0452A` 1.5px, no fill and no outline, so bay lines stay visible. Bay lines `#9FB8A9`.
- The only zoom-based change allowed: small labels (column clearances, bay numbers) fade in when zoomed in. They fade in place, never move, with a buffer (hysteresis) so they don't flicker at the threshold. Objects themselves never change.
- Dimension numbers in drawings: Plus Jakarta Sans 600, tabular figures.

## 5. Controls

- **Primary**: accent fill, on-accent text, 600. Heights: 36 in cards and modals, 28 in bars.
- **Ghost**: transparent, 1px separator border, text colour.
- **Inset**: inset fill, text colour (Depth, Fit, secondary bar actions).
- **Icon button**: 28–36 square, transparent, icon colour; active = active fill + accent icon.
- **Segmented**: inset track with 2–3px padding; active segment = active fill + accent text (or separator fill + strong text for neutral choices like Properties/Elevations).
- **Field**: inset fill, no border, radius 6; numbers mono 12; highlighted (being edited) = 1px select border. Heights: 30 in the right card, **26 in dialogs** (Project settings, Rules), **28 on New project**. Label above field: 11px muted, 6px gap.
- **Dialog spacing**: sections padded 20px 24px (New project 20px 28px), 14px between fields in a row and between rows. Chips 24h.
- **Toggle**: 30×18, off `#3A403C`, on accent with on-accent knob.
- **Scrollbars (everywhere, every scrolling area)**: thin, 8px; thumb `#3A403C` radius 4 with a 2px `#1F2321` border, hover `#4A514D`; track transparent. Firefox: `scrollbar-width: thin; scrollbar-color: #3A403C transparent`. Set once globally, never per component; no browser-default scrollbar anywhere.
- **Icons**: see section 9 (box style, 24px).
- **Hover** (island buttons): background `#2A2F2C` (inset); an active button (pressed tool, on toggle, open section) hovers `#355A47`.
- **Hover in the right card**: list rows, the More row, Check items, secondary (ghost), inset and danger buttons → background `#2A2F2C`. No hover change on toggles, colour swatches, the segmented control's active segment, a picked row or the mint primary button.
- **Keys while typing**: with focus in an input, select or textarea, Ctrl/Cmd + A C V X Z Y, Delete / Backspace, the arrows and the single-letter tool keys act on the text only, never on the canvas.
- **Disabled**: the same control at 45% opacity, cursor not-allowed.
- **Tool shortcuts** (shown in the tool pill's hint, key in mono; the Section menu's R and P only while it is open): V Select · P Pan · F Fill racking · G Row group · S Split · M Measure · L Line · T Text. **?** opens the shortcut sheet (§1 bottom-right).
- **Elevation full screen**: D toggles Front | Depth while full screen is open (never while typing, never with Ctrl / Cmd / Alt).

Row group keeps Skip beside Apply to N; the −N positions chip is the apply's narrow-aisle loss. ✕ and Esc leave every mode.

## 7. Behaviour rules

- Generate opens **inside the right card**. Top bar label: "Generate" on an empty plan, "Regenerate" when racks exist; Regenerate shows the warning line "Keeps zones, doors and columns. Replaces racking."
  - Fields: Building width · Building length / Column grid (its own row) / Max run · Wall clearance; Row direction; Columns along wall (Yes · No); Forklift. No Clear height (Generate never uses one). Forklift and Wall clearance are the project's — changed here they are a draft, written to Project settings only when Generate / Regenerate runs; Cancel or X drops them.
  - The warning line also says the hand edits a regenerate loses. The blue note gives the real sources: "Pallet 48×40 comes from Project settings; frame 42″ and flue 9″ from Rules." — each name opens its dialog.
  - Footer: Cancel · Generate / Regenerate. X and Cancel go back to the card that was showing. **Start over with a new building** is in the layout menu (File), behind a confirm — never in this card.
  - A pick on the plan (an object, or a building's wall) closes the card and shows Properties for it; the draft goes with it.
- Empty plan shows a centred card: "Your building is ready" · Generate layout · Draw racks. Gone after the first rack.
- Elevations: Depth and full-screen buttons sit in the "In this layout" row (editor: in its header). The drawing has no buttons on it, except in edit-on-sheet mode below.
- Elevation full screen: a **Front | Depth** segmented control in its header, left of the close button — the neutral segment style (§5: inset track, active = separator fill + strong text), 28h. It shares state with the card's Depth button: switching in one shows the same view in the other. Shortcut D (§5). Edit on the sheet works in Front only: in Depth the pencil is disabled (title "Edit in Front view") and nothing on the drawing edits; Save and Cancel stay.
- Max load height = sprinkler height − 18″ (NFPA clearance below sprinklers). If no sprinkler height is set, use the ceiling − 18″. Sprinkler height can never be above the ceiling (field error in Project settings). Label: Max load height N (18″ below sprinklers), placed by the max load label rule below.
- **Max load label** (front and side view): just below the max load line; if that overlaps any pallet or rack, it moves above the line inside the yellow band; if it still does not fit the width, the short form Max load N. It never overlaps a load or runs past the sheet. (Where a load reaches through the band so neither side of the line is clear in place, the label slides along the line to clear space.)
- Elevation rows: pencil (24×24) left of More. Pencil = **edit on the sheet** (C21, `StudioSheet`): the list collapses to that row, the sheet grows (560 at 900 tall), the row shows ✕ Cancel and a 24h Save. On the sheet: drag a beam (snaps to Project settings → Uprights → Hole pitch, default 2″; a text-only "2″ steps" hint over the dragged beam's label), click any blue value to type it (input box on the drawing), + Add level ghost box above the top load, − on the top level removes it. Editable values have a dotted blue underline. A load above max load height turns that level red. Save = one undo step, Esc = Cancel.
- More menu: Full edit (C3 with this elevation's values, title Edit elevation · N bays), Colour, Duplicate, Set as default, Remove unused elevations, Delete.
- Elevations list: **Apply** stays in place — "Select rows to apply", dimmed at 45%, with no row selected; "Apply to N selected rows" with rows (the footer never jumps). Fit is a double-click on the sheet.
- C3 (New and Full edit): every value is a typed input, no dropdowns or presets: Name, Ceiling, Pallet Depth / Width / Height / Weight, Rack Frame depth / Beam size / Beam length. The frame warning sits inside the Rack box.
- Pallet sizes always read depth × width (48×40).
- Right card width: drag its left edge (6px hit area, 3×32 grip `#4A514D` shown in C21) between 300 and 640; double-click resets to 300; remembered per user; same width for every tab.
- Elevation drawing labels (panel): Ceiling · Max load height (sprinklers −N) · free headroom + top of load stacked in one column on the far left · Frame dimension · beam heights at each beam (right) · L tags inside right pallet · pallet height and width arrows inside L1 pallets · face (4″) and lift-off (6″) as extension lines on the left of L1 · Beam length · pallets · capacity under the rack.
- **One elevation drawing.** The card's sheet is the reference. Full screen draws it scaled up (same labels, positions and colours) on the same white gridded sheet, with edit on the sheet there too: pencil, beam drag with the hole pitch snap, typed values, + Add level, −, Save, Esc cancels then closes. PDF elevation sheets draw it on paper: white, square corners (the sheet's radius 8 and grid are screen only), its front view and its side view at one architectural scale. There is no Simple | Detailed switch: one drawing, one label set. The per-level install chain (pallet, gap and beam face at every level) may return later as an **Install guide** export option — not on screen.
- **BOM (v1, round 2)** — selective single rows and back-to-back pairs, quantities only, no prices (utils/bom.js: the layout and its elevations in, lines out; never the screen). Upright frames per frame position (a shared upright once; a pair side's frame only beside a bay of that side) by height × depth; beams per bay side × beam levels, counted one by one with their pairs beside ("Beam 96″ × 4″ face: 18 (9 pairs)" — in the tab, the full view, the CSV, the xlsx and the PDF) — the elevation decides: a level whose height is above 0 has a beam, level 1 at 0 sits on the floor with no beam; the BOM counts exactly the beams the elevation sheet (card, full screen, PDF) draws. On the sheet level 1 is dragged or typed up from the floor to add its beam (snapped to the hole pitch), or typed 0 to remove it. Check layout's fit rule measures every level from its beam top; loads are drawn at their full height, never trimmed — one that runs into the beam above shows red — by length × face; safety pins 4 per beam pair; wire decks per beam level from Project settings' deck table (96″ 46+46, 108″ 52+52, 144″ 46+46+46 by default; a length with no row: none, and the flag "Set decks for N in beams") by width × depth; row spacers back-to-back only, between the two uprights of each frame pair: 1 per pair up to a frame height (Project settings, default 16 ft), 2 above, by flue; anchors 2 per frame × per foot (1); upright and end-of-aisle guards off unless set (row ends facing an aisle). Overlaps never count twice; bays with X marks count; a bay side with no elevation counts with the default, flagged. The **BOM tab** (right card, beside Properties and Elevations): the buttons **Tally** (primary) · **xlsx** · **CSV**, the flags in the warning style, then the **Total card** first (the active fill #2E4A3C, radius 8: Total, and on the right R1–Rn · N lines — N rack lines), then **one card per section** (1px #323835 border, radius 8; a #252A27 header band with the section's colour swatch, its name, its R range · N lines and a chevron — a click on the header collapses or expands it; the first section open, the rest collapsed, remembered per layout), **Unassigned** last, only when it holds racks; with no section drawn, the Total card alone. Each card lists its parts one per line — the part on one line ("Frame 21 ft x 42 in"), its quantity on the right in mono, beams with "(N pr)" muted beside; 8px between cards. A click on a part shades its racks on the plan (again: off). **xlsx** saves the By row table, **CSV** the Summary; the proposal PDF adds the table after the elevation sheets, in the paper palette.
- **Rack line numbers** — every rack line gets R1, R2 … (utils/rackLines.js): section by section, in the Sections list's order, then Unassigned (a line is in the drawn section holding its middle) — one continuous run, so adding or deleting rows in a later section never renumbers an earlier one; inside each (with no section drawn, the whole building), left to right for rows running up and down, top to bottom for rows running across (then left to right along one row line), each building in turn. A line is the racks of one kind standing end to end in one line (butting or sharing an upright; a cross-aisle starts a new line); a single row is one line, a back-to-back pair two (its left / top side first, the two numbers one after the other). Always sequential with no gaps, R1 to Rn, recomputed from position whenever rows are added, deleted or moved; the plan's labels and the BOM read the same computation. Shown in the BOM and on the plan: ONE label per rack line, just before its start end only — the left end of a line running across, the top end of one running up and down. A back-to-back pair running across gets one label per line, each beside its own line (R16 by the top line, R17 by the bottom one); a pair running up and down keeps one label for both, "R2–R3", at its top. Drawn like the aisle width labels — the same size rule, a size in feet (the Label size; drawings' words: Plus Jakarta Sans, #767C74) — and, like them, always drawn: never hidden when zoomed out. A label never overlaps another or a dimension label (the aisle and cross-aisle width labels): it slides along the line's end, else it is left out; hidden only with the Racking layer or the View card's Row numbers off.
- **BOM by section** — every part is counted on its rack line; a line belongs to the drawn section holding its middle, else Unassigned. The tab, the full view's Summary, the CSV and the PDF list each section's lines and totals (its rows, "R1–R4, R7"), then Unassigned, then the grand Total; with no section drawn, the Total alone. A pair's row spacers are split across its two lines: 2 per pair, 1 per frame on each line; 1 per pair, on the pair's first line. Guards: an upright guard on each end frame's line, the end-of-aisle guard on the pair's first line.
- **Tally** — the BOM tab's Tally opens the **drawer** at the bottom of the canvas (§1; it replaces the full-view modal as the default). Header: Tally · Summary / By row (By row first) · the hint "Hover a column or click a rack to match them" · **Pop out** (the wide modal below, in the drawer's place) · **xlsx** · ✕; Esc closes it (and the modal). By row as the modal's: the section labels over their rows (LEFT · R1–R9), the **Bays** row first (each line's counted bays, the section totals, the grand total), then the parts down — no pairs row — in two columns, **Part** (bold #F2F4F1: "Frame") and **Size** (muted #9AA29C, mono 11.5: "21 ft x 42 in"); compact R columns (min 36px) so more lines fit before it scrolls; a Total per section and the grand Total highlighted (active fill); it scrolls sideways when wide and Part and Size stay. **Summary** has the BOM tab's structure: the Total first (active fill band), then each section under a #252A27 header band (its swatch, name, R range), the parts in Part | Size | Qty columns, 12px between groups. The plan stays usable above it; on opening, the plan fits into the room above it (Fit, while it is open, too); the R labels show while it is open. **Linked to the plan:** an R column under the pointer lights that rack line on the plan — filled #D7F0E2 (multiplied over the racks, so their bays show), a 2px #2E7D5B outline — with a small tag over it, "R6 · 12 bays · 48 positions" (the line's counted bays on its side and their pallet positions); a click on a rack on the plan lights its column (a pair's bay picked on one side: that side's line; else every line of the rack) and scrolls it into view; a click on a section's Total column lights every line of that section (again: off). **Tallied marks:** each R column header in By row has a check toggle; a checked line's column dims (40%) with a green #2E7D5B tick in its header, and on the plan a small green tick stands just before its R label (a label naming two lines, when both are checked); the header reads **N / M checked** (M the lines) and **Clear checks** clears them all. The marks are saved with the layout (on its elevation table, so saved, loaded and undone with it; one undo step each). A line's mark goes by itself when its bays, levels, beams or depth change (a bay added or deleted, a side's bay emptied, a beam or an elevation changed); the other marks stay.
- **BOM full view** (the Tally's Pop out) — a Summary / By row switch (Summary first); both as the drawer's (Bays row, Part | Size, the banded Summary). **Download xlsx** saves the same By row table (the Bays row first, Part and Size as shown, no pairs row). By row, like a dealer's sheet: the parts down (frames by size, beams by length with their pairs on the row under, decks by size, row spacers, guards if on, anchors — no pins), R1 … Rn across grouped by section, a Total per section and a Grand total. **Download xlsx** saves the By row table in that layout (the sections over their rows); **Download CSV** the Summary (section, rows, part, size, qty, pairs).
- **Regenerate is one undo step**: the racks and the Forklift / Wall clearance it wrote come back together with one Ctrl+Z (Generate, and Project settings' regenerate, likewise).
- Exports and printing always use the paper palette, never the dark chrome.
- **Racking areas are invisible by default.** A racking area (from Fill racking or Regenerate) is drawn faintly as the Fill box (fill `#7BD3A4` at 6%, edge `#2E7D5B` 1.5 dashed 6 4) only while Fill racking is on, or while the area is hovered or selected; it is picked on its edge. Esc after a fill leaves nothing drawn. **Ctrl+A** selects only real layout objects (racks, zones, walls, doors, columns, equipment, text, dimensions, shapes), never helpers: the building outline, aisles, racking areas. Deleting every rack of a racking area deletes the area too.
- **Pan is the home tool**, as before: a plan opens with it, and ending any other tool (Done, Esc, a bar's ✕, a library item placing, a split) goes back to it. With Pan a click selects what is under the cursor, a click on the blank floor inside the building selects the building, and a drag pans; Esc or a click outside the building clears the selection. With Select (V) a drag on the floor box-selects rows. (This replaces "the floor never selects the building".)
- **One selection rule for the whole app: a selection is rows or bays, never both.**

  | Action | Rows selected (or nothing) | Bays selected |
  |---|---|---|
  | Click a rack | Selects its row (its section) | Selects that row; a click on a row whose bays are picked picks the bay under it |
  | Click the same selected row again (no movement) | Selects that bay | The same bay again: back to its row |
  | Press and drag on the selected row (a pair from its flue too) | Moves it, at the row level — never drops to bays | — |
  | Shift+click | Adds or removes rows | Adds or removes bays, in any row; on a selected bay, takes it off (a set: never one bay twice) |
  | Click the blank floor inside the building | Selects the building | Selects the building |
  | Drag on the floor | Pan: pans. Select (V): a box selects rows (Shift+drag adds rows) | Pan: pans. Select: a box selects rows (Shift+drag: their rows plus the new ones) |
  | Drag inside the selected bay's row | (moves the rack) | Moves the rows of the selected bays, the bays staying selected (past 6 px — §7a Moving) |
  | Shift+drag along a row | Selects the run of bays it crosses (rows are replaced: never rows and bays) | Adds the run of bays it crosses to the bays selected |
  | Esc, or a click outside the building | Clears the selection | Esc: steps back up to their rows |
  | Delete | Deletes the rows | Deletes only those bays; the rows stay. The only bay of a rack: deletes the row (one undo step, a notice with Undo) |
  | Look | Blue outline, no bay highlight | Bay filled; its row a thin dashed outline |

  | Flue of a back-to-back pair | First click: the pair (row level) | Second click at the same spot: the two facing bays (bay level) |
  | Dimension labels | One row: its labels. Several: none (the bar shows the count) | The row's labels |

  **Columns** follow the same drill-in: a click on a column selects that one column, like a bay, even with the Columns layer locked (locked means it cannot be moved, not that it cannot be clicked). A column picked: only its square is outlined; its bar's Show clearances (on when it is picked) shows that column's numbers only. The same column clicked again, or Esc, steps up to the whole grid; Esc again clears. A locked column is never moved or deleted (drag, arrows, Delete, Cut); a drag from a locked column is the floor's box. Delete with one column picked deletes nothing: a column stays with its grid (Esc, then Delete, deletes an unlocked grid). Ctrl+A still skips locked layers.

  A drag on the floor outside the building, or with Space or the middle button, always pans.
- **Row group** (§7a Modes, which this follows): a click on a row not in the group adds it; a click on a group row selects the bay under the pointer (bay level, as outside Row group), so its width and other bay fields are edited in the card and the edit applies to the same bay position in the other group rows (Ask or Auto apply as set); Shift+click on a group row removes it; a drag on empty floor adds the rows in the box. No edit row and no selection bar: every group row shows its handles, a handle drag edits that row and applies to the others, a body drag moves the whole group. Mode bar hint: "Click rows to add · click a bay to edit · Shift+click to remove". The purple group outline shows only while the Row group tool is on: **Done** (primary, 28 high, at the right of the mode bar, before ✕), Esc, ✕ or picking any other tool ends the mode — the outline gone at once, nothing selected, no bar. The tool pill or G start it empty; the selection bar's Row group starts it from the selection.
- **A back-to-back pair is ONE object.** Deleting a bay on one side marks that bay position empty on that side (`bayEmpty` on the pair, utils/bayEmpty.js); it never cuts the side into separate objects. The pair becomes two objects only when both facing bays at a position are deleted. An empty side position is drawn as an empty bay (no bay lines on that side, outline kept), holds no pallet positions, and the BOM counts no beams for it but keeps the shared uprights (a frame beside a full bay stays; one between two empty bays goes). Moving, rotating, the Row group and the labels treat the pair as one object. On load, the split pieces of an older layout are joined back into one pair when they line up on the same row and side and share their end upright — unless the lost half would stand on a zone, past the walls or on another rack, or had no aisle in front of it (the generator cuts such a half off on purpose, and that single is picked from behind). A single row with a middle bay deleted still splits. Selection, the bar and the labels still read pieces that touch end to end as one section.
- **The selection bar never covers a label**: its own dimensions, aisle labels, column clearances. It tries above, then below, then slides sideways along the object.
- **Column clearance numbers** (PlanDetail C18: a small grey `#767C74` number, `8′-8″`, on a thin dotted `#9AA29C` line, no arrowheads; touching a face = the number only, beside the column) show when zoomed in past the label threshold — the 9.5 number at least 8 px tall on screen, staying until it is under 7 px (the buffer), fading in and out in place — when that column is picked (its numbers only) or the grid is selected, or Show clearances is on, or during a drag or resize. A number is hidden when the gap it labels is narrower than the number itself, so a number never overlaps a rack or column (the same rule will apply to bay numbers). The red "under travel" warnings stay with the Checks layer. Columns are solid `#1F2321` squares at true size. The PDF keeps printing every number.
- **The building is resized only from its handles**: the corner and side squares shown while it is selected, each hit within 8 screen px (no larger). A press on a wall or an edge selects or moves the building, never resizes it. Shrinking it removes the bays that no longer fit inside the new walls with the wall clearance — whole bays from the row ends; rows fully outside are removed — while zones and columns left outside are flagged in Check layout, not deleted; one undo step, notice "N bays removed" with Undo. Enlarging adds nothing and shows no notice.
- **Add door** (the building bar) is a tool: hover any wall on any side — a door preview snaps to the inner wall face — click to place (one undo step), the tool stays on, Esc ends it. Mode bar: Add door · type segmented Man door · Dock door · Drive-in door · Width (the type's, or typed) · the hint · ✕. The library's doors: Man Door 3′ (was Egress Door), Dock Door 9′ (was Loading Dock Door), Drive-in Door 12′ overhead (`struct_drive_in`, wall-snapped like the others). Not in the library: Fire door, Warehouse door.
- **Notices**: plain info fades after 5 s, a notice with Undo after 8 s; the pointer over a notice pauses its timer; questions (Continue / Cancel) stay until answered.
- **Bar buttons**: the mint primary (Apply, Continue, Export) hovers slightly lighter, never darker; ghost buttons (Skip, Cancel, Undo, a chip) hover a step lighter; disabled is 45% and still readable.

## 7a. Interaction rules

Frozen for the Studio build. It replaces any earlier selection rule that disagrees: if something in code or an older rule disagrees, 7a wins; if 7a is unclear, stop and ask. Each rule is a test in `src/__test__/plan/REG_handCheck.test.js` (the REG-7a matrix).

- **Tools:** Pan is the default; click selects what is under the cursor, drag pans. Select (V): click selects; a drag that starts on empty floor or canvas box-selects rows (and the zones, doors and text it touches — see Levels); a drag that starts on an object moves it. Space+drag or middle-drag pans in any tool. Hover highlights exactly what a click would select.
- **Moving:** plain press and drag on a rack or on the current selection moves it, keeping the selection (rows, or the rows of the selected bays). A move starts only after 6 screen px of travel; under that it is a click. Shift+drag along a row selects a run of bays and adds it to the current bay selection. (There is no plain-drag bay run.)
- **Levels:** never mix rows with bays, or one column with others (the building is selected alone too). Different object kinds can mix: racks, zones, doors and text can be selected together with Shift+click or a box, and moved, deleted, duplicated together. The bar shows only actions all of them share, plus the count ("N selected · Lock · Duplicate · Delete").
- **Blank floor inside the building:** click selects the building. Drag: Pan pans, Select box-selects rows. Esc clears.
- **Canvas outside the building:** click clears. Drag: Pan pans, Select box-selects rows.
- **Wall:** click selects the building. Drag moves the building with everything inside it (racks, zones, columns, doors). Delete removes the building only; contents stay; notice with Undo. A selected wall is shown by a 3 px #2E7D5B line over the wall; its dimension label stays blue.
- **Building handles:** only the handles resize (8 px hit area); a press on a wall or edge never resizes. Shrink removes bays that no longer fit with wall clearance, and shrinks the racking area with it: clipped to the new inner walls minus the wall clearance; an area left with no space is removed. A back-to-back pair left with one face against the new wall (no aisle in front of it) loses that dead half; the single row left moves flush to the wall at the wall clearance, as Generate places wall rows, with no X marks. One undo, notice with Undo counting the bays removed. Enlarge adds nothing and shows no notice (Fill new space is removed); the resize is one undo.
- **Row or section:** click selects the row. Click again without moving selects that bay. Shift+click adds or removes rows. Press and drag moves the row, selected or not, never drops to bay level. Delete deletes the rows.
- **Flue of a pair:** click selects the pair; click again selects the two facing bays; drag moves the pair. Two facing bays picked one by one are two items in the set; Shift+click removes only the one clicked.
- **Bay level:** click selects that bay; Shift+click adds or removes bays in any row, selection is a set; Shift+drag along a row adds a run of bays (see Moving); a plain drag on a row of the selected bays moves those rows, the bays staying selected; Esc goes up to the row; Delete deletes those bays, and the only bay of a rack deletes the row.
- **Column:** click selects that one column, even on the locked Columns layer, and shows its clearances; click again or Esc goes up to the grid; Delete on one column shows a notice that it stays with its grid. **Column lines** (only when the Columns layer is unlocked; locked, a drag from a column moves nothing): drag a column sideways and its whole vertical grid line moves (every column on that line); up or down, its horizontal line. The axis is decided by the first 6 px of movement; the distances to the neighbouring lines show live while dragging. A click on a column shows in the card the distance to the previous and next line in each direction as typed fields; typing moves that column's line. Other lines stay put; one undo per move; the column clearances and checks update.
- **Zone:** click selects; Shift+click adds or removes zones; drag moves it and removes racks under it without asking; Delete deletes. While dragging or resizing a zone, it is checked live: if it blocks an aisle, cuts off rack faces or leaves any zone with no way in, the preview turns red with the reason before drop. A drop is allowed. Check layout (clarified by the user, 2026-11-04): faces short of the pick width and aisles a zone closes keep their X marks and warnings as before; the one new error is a zone with no way in (no access from any aisle), whatever placed it — staging needs the truck's travel width, an office, washroom or custom area a walkway (Rules → Zones → Walkway, 44″ by default — the common egress corridor minimum; the dealer confirms local code).
- **Door:** click selects; Shift+click adds or removes doors; drag slides it along its wall; Delete deletes.
- **Pairs:** a back-to-back pair is one object; deleting a bay on one side leaves that position empty on that side; it splits only when both facing bays at a position are gone.
- **Modes (Row group, Split, Fill, Door):** mode bar at top; Done, Esc, X or another tool ends the mode, clears the purple outline, selects nothing. Fill ends after one fill: on mouse up the racks are placed and the tool returns to Pan, no Esc needed; Esc before mouse up cancels the box (nothing placed); to fill again, pick the tool again (F). Row group: a click on a row not in the group adds it; a click on a group row selects the bay under the pointer (bay level, as outside Row group) — its width and other bay fields edited in the card apply to the same bay position in the other group rows (Ask or Auto apply as set); Shift+click on a group row removes it; a drag on empty floor adds every row inside the box. No edit row: every group row shows its resize handles, and dragging any handle edits that row and applies to the others (Ask or Auto apply as set). Press and drag on a group row's body past 6 px moves the whole group, one undo; under 6 px it is a click (the bay under it). Cursor: move over a group row body, resize over handles, crosshair over empty floor. A click on empty floor does nothing; the group stays.
- **Sections:** a section is a named area drawn on the plan with one setting, its aisle **Equipment** (below) — no racking settings, no Generate change; racks work exactly as without it. **Draw:** the tool pill's Section ▾ (after Pan): Rectangle (R) — drag a box; Custom shape (P, the pen) — click points, a double-click or a click on the first point closes it. Mode bar: Section · Rectangle | Custom shape · the hint (Drag to draw a section · Pen: click points, double-click to close) · ✕. One undo per section; the tool returns to Pan with the new section selected; Esc drops a drawing in progress. **Look:** under the racks — with no colour, no fill and a grey #9AA29C 1px dashed outline (dash 6 4); selected (or hovered in the Sections list) the green #2E7D5B 1.5px outline only, no tint; with resize handles (a rectangle) or point handles (a custom shape). Its label is **one dark tag** — the zone size tag's dark pill (JetBrains Mono 11 px), its text white #F2F4F1 — reading **Name · W × L** in the app's feet-inches, as other dimensions ("Section 1 · 137′-5″ × 87′-3″"), or, for a custom shape, **Name · area** ("Section 2 · 2,625 sq ft"). **Placement:** on open floor, inside the section along each edge in turn — bottom, right, top, left (the middle of the edge first, then along it) — the first spot where the whole tag has no rack, R label, aisle label (its arrow too), zone or other tag under it; with none inside, just outside an edge (same order, sliding past a corner) on open floor (the building's, no other section); never over racks or labels. With no room anywhere, a section hovered or selected still shows its tag, just outside its top edge (centred, over whatever is there, drawn on top); only the Section names toggle (every section) skips a tag that has no room. No name text inside the section and no dimension labels along its edges. The tag shows while the section is hovered or selected, or for every section while the View card's **Section names** is on; a hidden tag is floor; a press on a shown tag is the section's. The Sections list, the BOM and the PDF (the name at the top-left inside) are unchanged. **Select:** a click on its outline, its tag (while it shows — over whatever is under it) or its empty area selects it (a click on a rack inside still selects the rack); selected, a press and drag inside it (past 6 px) moves it with its racks; not selected, a drag in its empty area is the floor's (Pan: pans). Card: Name (typed), Width and Length (a rectangle) or Area (a custom shape). A drag moves the section only, never its racks; Delete removes the section only. A second click on a selected section's tag renames it in place (Enter writes, Esc drops). **Names:** Section 1, 2, 3 — one past the highest. Sections touch but never overlap: a draw over a neighbour snaps to the neighbour's edge (a pen point inside one, to its nearest edge). **Membership:** a rack belongs to the section that holds its centre, else Unassigned — worked out live, so it follows every move. **Card with nothing selected:** a Sections list in the Layout summary (Summary follows selection, below) — each section's colour swatch, name, rows and positions, then Unassigned; a row's hover lights its section on the plan, a click selects it. **Layers:** sections are on their own Sections layer (show and lock in the Layers card); Ctrl+A takes them like any object; a lock stops changes, never a click. **Like any object:** drag, the arrow keys, Shift+click, the right-click menu, and a selection bar: Colour ▾ · **Fill section** (the whole section filled with its equipment in one click, one undo; clipped to it) · Lock · Duplicate (a copy beside it — right, below, left or above, the first spot inside the building clear of every section — with the next name) · Delete. A drag or an arrow moves the section **with the racks whose centre is in it** (and the doors in its Fence or Wall), one undo. **Kept in:** a section stays inside its building — drawing (a rectangle's drag, the pen's points), a drag and a resize all stop at the inner wall face — and never overlaps another: they stop at the neighbour's actual edge, its real shape, never its box (an L-shaped section and a rectangle in its notch touch with no overlap). A rectangle started inside a neighbour starts on that neighbour's nearest edge. **Typed size:** a rectangle's Width and Length are typed in the card (one undo, the top-left stays, stopped at the wall and the neighbours). **Pen helpers:** a segment snaps to horizontal, vertical or 45° (within 6°) with a dashed guide and its live length beside the pointer; Shift locks it to the nearest of them; a point within 8 screen px of a wall's inner face or a section edge lands on it. **Wall close:** when the pen started on a wall and a later click lands on a wall, drawing stops: both closing sides — along the walls each way — are previewed, the one under the pointer lit, and the next click picks it (the hint: Click the side to keep · Esc cancels); Esc cancels. A side that would overlap another section is not offered. **Colour:** 8 soft colours (Sage, Sky, Sand, Rose, Lilac, Teal, Peach, Slate) or none, in the card and the bar; the outline, the tint and the label take it, and the PDF. **Boundary** (the card): None (an area only), Fence (a thin line, a wire partition), Wall (its thickness typed, 6″ by default). With a Fence or a Wall, its selection bar shows **Add door**, which starts the door tool on that section's edges only; the door tool from anywhere else snaps to the section's edges as well as the building's walls, and the door cuts that edge (the boundary is drawn around it, on the plan and in the PDF); such a door slides along that edge and moves with the section. **Fill racking** started inside a section is clipped to it: no rack it places crosses the section's edge (a custom shape keeps only the racks wholly inside). With a section **selected**, a Fill drag can start anywhere: the fill is clipped to the selected section and uses its equipment. **Snapping:** while a section is drawn (its corners), moved or resized (the edges the handle moves), its edges snap onto other sections' edges, the building's inner walls, zone edges and rack ends (a rack's two ends along its run) within 8 screen px — the nearest per axis — with a dashed guide through the matched edge; it is then kept in the building and off its neighbours as before. Alt held: no snap (the grid's neither). **Generate / Regenerate** (the top bar, the Generate card, Project settings' Regenerate racking) is off while the building holds 2 or more sections — titled "Fill each section with Fill racking"; with 0 or 1 it works as before. **Check layout** warns when racks in two sections face each other across a section edge with less than the aisle width: "Section 1 and Section 2: racks face each other across the section edge 8′-6″ apart, needs 10′-6″ aisle".
- **Aisle and gap labels with sections:** a width label (an aisle's, a cross-aisle's, a face's in front of a wall or zone) never crosses a section edge — it measures only between racks in the same section (or both in none). Between two sections (the gap at the label crosses a section's edge, or runs through a section) only a real aisle is labelled: racks facing each other across the edge (an aisle's two rows), closer than 3 × the travel width (Space to move) of the stricter equipment of the two sides; a cross-aisle (run ends) or a face across an edge gets none. Anywhere, a gap wider than 3 × that travel width is open floor, not an aisle: no label (the reach truck's 8′: over 24′). A label not shown is not there to pick, and the PDF follows the plan.
- **Section sizes:** a section's size is on its tag (Sections, above) — no dimension labels along its edges; the View card's Section sizes switch is gone, folded into **Section names**. Hidden with the Sections layer.
- **Aisle equipment:** a section's card has **Equipment** (after Name): Project forklift (the default, named with the project's forklift), any of the forklift list — Reach truck, VNA / turret, Counterbalance, Pallet jack, Walkie stacker — or **Custom**. Each has two widths: **Aisle to pick** (turn and put away) and **Space to move** (drive straight). Defaults: Reach truck 10′ 6″ / 8′, VNA / turret 7′ / 6′, Counterbalance 12′ 6″ / 8′, Pallet jack 7′ / 5′ (60″; minimum 6′ 6″), Walkie stacker 8′ 6″ / 8′ (minimum 8′). Project settings' **forklift list** (under Forklift and walls) types both for each truck, for this project, over the Rules; an emptied field goes back to the Rules' value. **Custom** shows the two fields in the card (they start from the widths the section was held to) and the section's checks use exactly those two; its minimum (the Fill bar's amber line) is its aisle to pick. Saved with the section. The aisle checks of a rack use its section's equipment (the section holding its centre); a rack in no section uses the equipment its racking area was designed for (a Fill's or a Generate's, stored on the area — below), else the project's forklift (racks placed by hand, areas from before); an aisle between two racks on different equipment is held to the stricter (the wider pick aisle). This covers Check layout's aisle items (drive, cross-aisle, pick, no way in, nobody can reach it), the aisle width and cross-aisle labels' colours (each label graded against its two rows' equipment, found on the whole layout — an aisle exactly the equipment's aisle to pick passes), the X marks and usable positions of faces under the pick width, and the column check's markings on the plan (a column in an aisle, columns lost) — so the plan and the list always agree.
- **Fill racking's Aisle — one source:** a section's aisle is its Equipment, set in its card (default the project's forklift); drawing, moving or dropping a section asks nothing. A fill started inside a section (the bar follows the pointer: over a section, or the drag started in one) shows the Aisle read-only — the section's equipment and width, e.g. "VNA / turret · 7 ft (from section)" — and is laid out for it; to change it, change the section's Equipment. Outside every section the Aisle is typed as before (the project's forklift's by default; a length typed is for that fill only), and a typed aisle under the project forklift's minimum shows an amber line in the bar before the drop: "7′ 0″ is below Reach truck minimum 10′ 0″. Needs its own section." After every fill outside a section a notice says **Racks placed · Make section**: with a normal aisle it fades after 8 s like other notices with an action (hover pauses it), and Make section makes a section of the fill box on the project's forklift, in the fill's own undo step. **Equipment, a design per fill:** outside every section the bar has **Equipment** — Project forklift | Custom. Project forklift lays the fill out on its widths (the Aisle typed as before; a typed aisle under its minimum still shows the amber line and errors; the amber line has **Use Custom**, which switches Equipment to Custom with that aisle as its Aisle to pick and Space to move). Custom shows **Aisle to pick** and **Space to move** (optional — empty, as much as the aisle to pick; Custom starts at the project forklift's aisle): the fill is laid out on them (the aisle to pick between rows, the space to move for cross-aisles, travel paths and the gap at a run end) and its racks are checked against them. Inside a section the bar has no Equipment: the Aisle stays read-only from the section, as before. Each fill's racking area keeps the equipment it was made for (`equip`): a Custom design its two widths, kept as stored; a Project forklift fill a reference to the project forklift, not its widths — Generate stores the same reference — so changing the project forklift rechecks those racks at once. **Which equipment a rack is checked against** — Check layout, the aisle label colours and the X marks: its section's equipment when it is in a section (a section on the project forklift too); else the equipment its racking area was designed for; else the project's forklift (racks placed by hand). A Custom design shows in the layout summary (nothing selected) and on the PDF's Layout check page as **Designed for 6 ft aisles, equipment to be confirmed** — one line per design. There is no "Make this a section?" question any more: after every fill outside a section the fading **Racks placed · Make section** notice (Make section after a Custom fill makes a Custom section with its widths). **Placement along the rows:** when the fill box touches a wall at one run end (a zone's edge counts as a wall), every row starts flush at the wall clearance there and the leftover — less than a bay — goes to the open end; touching no wall at either run end, the rows start at the box's left edge (rows running across) or top edge (rows up and down) and the leftover goes to the other end, regardless of where the drag started; never centred. Touching walls at both run ends, the rows start at the end the drag started from, as before. At the open end the rows stay inside the drawn box (no bay past its edge); against racks beyond that end they come as close as the required cross-aisle — the cross-aisle (else the space to move) of the stricter equipment of the two sides, each side's section's (else the project's forklift) — instead of the forklift's pick aisle, so a box drawn up to those racks gets the extra whole bay when the gap still meets that width, and not when it would not. Never into another section, a zone or the wall clearance. Every row of the fill follows the same rule, so the rows stay lined up; the racking area keeps the end its rows start from, and a resize or rebuild walks from it again.
- **Check layout — Mark as OK:** each item has Mark as OK. A marked item stays in the list, greyed (45 %), at the bottom, tagged Marked as OK, with Undo mark; it no longer counts (the counts, the Check label, the export question). The marks are saved with the layout (on its elevation table) and undone with it. The PDF ends with a Layout check page: the errors, the warnings, and the marked items listed as accepted.
- **Summary follows selection** (the right card's Properties tab only): under whatever is selected, the layout's figures in blocks — each an 11px uppercase muted caption, mono numbers, 12px padding, a #2E3431 line between blocks. **Nothing selected:** the Layout summary, in this order — **Storage**: positions, usable (accent), lost (#FFB4A3); a thin usable vs lost bar; **Lost to** with its counts — Narrow aisle, Column, Zone, Other (the wall); positions per 1,000 sq ft (of the building's floor). **Building**: size, area, clear height, sprinklers (Project settings' default ceiling; not set: **Not set**, muted). **Racking**: rack lines, bays, frames; then one line per elevation in use — its colour swatch, its name, its bays. **Aisles**: the pick aisle width (the aisle labels shown; a range when they differ), the cross aisles' count and width, the equipment — the project forklift, then every section on other equipment and every Custom design (**Designed for N ft aisles, equipment to be confirmed**). **Sections**: the Sections list with each section's colour swatch. **Checks**: the error, warning and note counts as chips (the Check card's); **Open** opens the Check card. Then More. With something selected, the summary blocks after its own fields sit in one **Summary** fold, closed by default, its open state remembered (per browser); nothing selected, the full summary shows open, with no fold. **A building:** its fields, then the whole Layout summary. **A section:** its fields, then Storage, Racking, Aisles (its equipment) and Checks (the items naming its racks) for that section only. **A rack line or pair** (a rack clicked): its fields as before, then its lines' Storage, Racking (bays, frames, levels) and **Parts** — the line's mini BOM (a pair: its two lines'), each part its By row column. **Several racks** (or a group): their fields as before, then their totals (Storage; racks, bays, frames). **A bay:** its fields, then its positions and levels, and its lost positions when it is X-marked. A zone, a door or a column: no summary. **Every number is the BOM's, the Tally's or usable's own:** positions the bay ledger's (a line's, the Tally's tag), lost the blocks usable takes off (column, narrow aisle, zone, wall — the X marks, on the racks' own equipment), bays and frames the BOM's (a section's its BOM section's lines), parts the By row table's. A click on **Lost** lights the racks that lose positions on the plan (red, as the X marks; Check layout's highlight — it stays until the next click on the canvas; Lost again clears it).
- **Hit rule (one for every click):** an object whose real shape holds the click beats an object reached only by its pick padding — racks, walls, section outlines and shown names, doors, columns, zones alike. Among the objects that truly hold it, the one drawn on top wins; padding decides only when nothing truly holds the click (again the one on top); the building's blank floor (and a section's empty area) comes last. Real shapes: an object's own footprint; a column's square; a wall's drawn line (its thickness, 2 screen px at least); a section's or a racking area's outline line (2 screen px) and a section's shown name; an aisle's width label. Padding: a rack's few screen px around it, a column's 4, an outline's 6, a wall's band (24). The same rule decides the select, the hover, the right-click and the start of a drag, so a hover always shows what a click takes.
- **Global:** Ctrl+A selects everything, the building included (as before 5bdecf8); only layers the user locked by hand are skipped (a lock Generate set is not). Delete then clears all: one undo step, notice with Undo. Right-click an object selects it and opens its menu; right-click floor opens the floor menu. ## 8. How to build from this

1. One screen per prompt. Open the matching mockup file, match it exactly.
2. Screenshot your build at 1440×900 next to the mockup screenshot. List every difference. Fix until none. Put both images in the report.
3. Never invent a value. If something is missing here and in the mockup, stop and ask.
4. **Every hand-check bug becomes a REG test.** Each bug from a hand check gets a test in `src/__test__/plan/REG_handCheck.test.js` that drives a real click: the app in Chromium, real mouse and keys on the canvas (`handCheck/browser.js`), never a direct function call. REG_handCheck runs on the saved fixtures in `src/__test__/plan/handCheck/`: an **older layout** (saved by an older Generate) and a **crowded layout** (a rack along a wall, a rack beside a column, labels near the selection bar), each in both orientations. **Never delete a REG test**; when a rule changes, update the test's expectation and keep the test.

5. **Canvas overlays read live positions — one way.** Anything drawn over the plan (R labels, aisle labels, section tags, clearance numbers, the Tally highlight and tags, the Check / BOM highlight, the selection bar, the column-line gaps, the section rename box) gets positions only from `src/canvas2/live.js`: a Konva overlay draws in world px from `useLiveObjects()` (the store's objects with the current drag applied) and lets the Stage transform place it; a DOM overlay places itself with `useView()` (the transform the canvas applies, published in the same call). Never the store's saved objects or its pan/zoom during a pan or a drag: a drag writes the store only on the drop and the pan lands in the store a frame late, so such an overlay lags, then jumps. `LV_liveOverlays.test.js` (a source scan) and REG-live-overlays (real mouse) fail on any overlay that reads positions another way.

## 9. Icons (locked) — see design/studio/Icons.dc.html

One icon style everywhere: **D, box style.** Objects, rail categories, tools, selection-bar actions, top-bar actions and small buttons.

- Flat solid shapes, no outlines, on a 24px grid; objects sit in the middle ~16px.
- Straight edges on whole pixels (`shape-rendering: crispEdges` on rects); curves, diagonals and arrowheads stay smooth.
- Thin parts are 1px: walls, door wall stubs, forks, dimension lines, dashed boxes, row lines, cut lines, tray edges. Bodies 2–10px.
- Draw only at 24px or exactly 48px (2x). Never scale to other sizes.
- **Object colours** (on the dark UI): ops `#F59A45` (dark parts `#C46F22`, cab `#FFD5AE`); racks `#7BD3A4` with dark `#1F2321` bay gaps; structure `#C9CEC9` (stairs/conveyor `#8C948E`); safety yellow `#F5CD4A`, red `#F07A5F`, teal `#5FC4B8`, exit `#7BD3A4`; zones in their own colour; dimension `#7FA6FF`.
- **Tool / action colours:** idle `#C9CEC9`, active `#7BD3A4` on the active fill `#2E4A3C`. Cut-outs inside a solid shape use `#1F2321`.
- Forklift, reach truck and pallet jack always show two forks.
- The same thing always uses the same icon (rail, flyout row, selection bar, zone label).
- No other icon set. Not Lucide, not line icons.
- **Add bay** and **Remove bay** (the rack's selection bar): next to Duplicate and Delete in Icons.dc.html — a bay with a plus, a bay with a minus.
